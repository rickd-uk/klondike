# Court card art

`{J,Q,K}{C,D,H,S}.svg` are the court cards from Byron Knoll's
[Vector Playing Cards](https://code.google.com/archive/p/vector-playing-cards/),
released into the public domain, taken from the mirror at
https://github.com/notpeter/Vector-Playing-Cards (`cards-svg/`).

They are recoloured red, gold and black (no blue or green) and shrunk with svgo.
Rebuild with `bash tools/build-courts.sh`.
